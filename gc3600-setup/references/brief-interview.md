# Business Design Brief Interview

Use this only when the student does not have their brief written yet. Ask one
question at a time. Reflect their answer back in one sentence before moving on,
so they can correct you.

Keep their words. Do not upgrade "a bakery my aunt runs" into "an artisanal
patisserie brand". The brief belongs to them.

## The problem

1. What is the business or organization, in one sentence?
2. What problem does this website solve for them right now? What are they losing
   without it?

## The users

3. Who is the site for? Describe one real person who would land on it.
4. What is that person trying to do in the first thirty seconds?
5. What would make them leave immediately?

## Goals

6. If the site works, what happens? A call, a visit, a signup, a purchase, a
   download?
7. How would you know it worked?

## Requirements

8. What pages does the site need? Push back gently if they list more than five.
9. What content already exists, and what has to be created?
10. Anything that absolutely must be on the site? Hours, location, a menu, a
    portfolio, a contact form?

## Design direction

11. Three words for how the site should feel.
12. Any brand assets already? Logo, colors, fonts, photos?
13. Show me one site you like and one you do not. What specifically is different
    about them?

## Constraints

14. Is there a real client or is this hypothetical?
15. Anything you have already decided that is not up for debate?

## After the interview

Summarize what you heard in under 200 words and ask them to correct it. Only then
write CLAUDE.md.
